About ScoutMatch

ScoutMatch helps clubs and scouts match players to team needs based on playing style, position, and skill tags that way turning scouting into a fast, data-driven process instead of guesswork.

How It Works

1. Enter Player Attributes

Input a player's position, key skills, and playing style as tags.

2. Set Team Needs

Define what your team is looking for: pace, finishing, defensive work rate, and more.

3. Get a Match Score

ScoutMatch compares the tags and instantly calculates how well the player fits.
